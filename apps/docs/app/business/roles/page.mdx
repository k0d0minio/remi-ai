# Roles

> **Not yet written.** Scope reads this page to decide who a change serves; the pipeline's label
> vocabulary can grow a `persona:*` axis once these are real.

The shape it should take — one section per role:

## &lt;Role name&gt;

- **What they are trying to do** — in their words, not the product's
- **What they can see** — the surfaces and data in scope for them
- **What they can change** — and what needs someone else's say-so
- **Which app they live in** — `web`, `admin`, or both

Decision rights are the most common silent assumption in a scope. Writing them here once means a
scope document does not have to re-derive them every time.
