# Initiatives

The strategy the current work ladders up to, and what this period is measured against. Scope and
Define read this page for the why-now; Ship quotes an objective from it, verbatim, in the ship note.
The feature list the work is drawn from is [V2 scope](/business/scope).

## Current initiative

**A patient experience validated on real terrain, in time for the December open day.** REMI is
pre-launch: nothing is sold, no customer data is processed, there is no revenue and there is no
traction to report. What this period is trying to move is one question — does REMI actually help a
patient apply what their practitioner told them — and the horizon is the clinical partner's open
day on 19 December 2026, where the new version is presented and commercialisation begins.

The method is deliberate and it is the opposite of the previous plan. Rather than building the
complete product and confronting it with users afterwards, Morgane accompanies 10–15 of her own
patients now — with tools as simple as a messaging thread where that is enough — and each brick of
REMI is tested against that accompaniment as it is built. Terrain, test, feedback, build, test
again. Less is built, and what is built is built right.

Two consequences follow, and both are scope decisions rather than preferences:

- **The patient experience comes first, alone.** A practitioner encodes a patient's profile and
  recommendations in the patient's own space, which is what makes it possible to test the real
  product without a practitioner dashboard existing yet.
- **The practitioner space is parked** until the patient half is validated, and will then be
  specified from what Morgane has learned accompanying those patients — not from a specification
  written ahead of the experience.

> **There is no pilot, no pilot price and no billing date.** Earlier versions of this page carried
> a founding cohort with commercial terms, a ceiling and an enrolment window. None of it existed:
> it was demo fixture data in the operator console that an earlier audit read as a signed
> agreement. The ~15 practitioners in the braindump are a **beta recruitment target**, nobody has
> signed anything, and no price is decided — the braindump's own figures are written as
> _réflexions_. A stage that needs a commercial number does not have one to take.

## Objectives

| Objective                                                      | Measure                                                                                     | Where it stands                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The patient loop working end to end for one real patient       | A profile, the practitioner's recommendations, and a first micro-action inside a minute     | Both surfaces built on fixture data — nothing persists yet                |
| A usable patient version for the partner clinic to test        | Testable by their team on 1 December 2026                                                   | Not started as product; the terrain accompaniment is running              |
| The database and accounts under it                             | Supabase connected: real accounts, real records, the invite link between them               | Decided and unstarted — storage, email and AI are still uncommitted seams |
| The data question answered before the first real record exists | EU hosting, processing agreements and retention rules written down before processing        | Trust page live; nothing processes personal data yet                      |
| The build kept inside what self-funding allows                 | Every feature outside [V2 scope](/business/scope) is a no, and the estate is sized to match | The scope is frozen; the six-app footprint is under review                |

Where each of those actually stands, in more words than a cell holds:

- **The patient loop.** Today, meals, steps and the plan behind them are built, and so is the
  practitioner side of the same fixture data. Every screen reads through the query layer:
  registering a database replaces the body of each query and moves no caller.
- **The December version.** The terrain phase has started outside the product — real patients,
  simple tools, real observations. The product work that meets it is the patient core loop:
  onboarding, « améliore mon assiette », the daily hub, the supplement journal, micro-actions.
- **The database.** Supabase is the decision of record, named in the braindump and adopted here.
  The code was built so it could be plugged in without rework; that is now the next foundation.
- **The data question.** The trust posture is published on the public site. Storage, email and AI
  are still seams in `@remi/services` with no vendor committed — which is exactly what keeps data
  residency a selection criterion rather than a migration project.
- **The budget.** V2 is self-funded and deliberately capped, which is why the frozen scope is a
  gate rather than a wish list: anything outside it spends money the project does not have. The
  figure itself is not published here — a stage that needs it asks the owner.

## What this page does not claim

No users, no results, no revenue, no signed practitioners, no price. The three statistics on the
public site are WHO figures describing the problem REMI addresses, not outcomes REMI has produced,
and this page holds to the same rule: a number that cannot be sourced does not appear here, and
fixture data is not evidence.
