# V2 scope

The frozen feature list for V2, and the list of things that are explicitly not in it. Scope and
Define read this page to check that a request is inside the build; anything not named here is out
until the owner adds it.

Every line below is taken from Morgane's braindump — [`roadmap/features.md`][features],
[`roadmap/court-term.md`][court] and [`roadmap/priorities.md`][priorities]. Nothing is added to it.
The braindump is the source of truth for **what** REMI is; where sequencing is concerned it is
outranked by the direction of record (below), and the precedence order between all of those
documents is in [`.icm/docs/README.md`][precedence].

## The order the work is built in

The direction of record, adopted 27 August 2026, is terrain-first: build from what real patients
turn out to need rather than shipping a complete product and discovering the gaps afterwards.

- **The patient experience is built and validated first.** A patient can create a profile, carry
  the recommendations their practitioner gave them, and get personalised accompaniment from them —
  without a practitioner space existing yet. In this first phase the practitioner encodes a
  patient's profile and recommendations in the patient's own space.
- **The practitioner space comes after that is validated**, and is specified from Morgane's own
  experience of accompanying 10–15 patients, not from a spec written ahead of it. The practitioner
  features below stay in the V2 scope; they are not in the first phase.
- **Milestones.** A usable patient version for the clinical partner's team to test on
  **1 December 2026**; their feedback and corrections through 18 December; the partner's open day
  on **19 December 2026**, where the new version is presented and commercialisation begins.
- **Genotype-based personalisation is a live business track, not a build item.** Laboratories are
  being contacted; it is explicitly not to block the first patient version. The nutrigenomics
  engine stays out of scope — see below.

## Patient — the V2 features

| Feature                             | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ultra-simple onboarding**         | Fast and intuitive, built on the foods a patient eats most often, ending in a first micro-action generated immediately. The 7-day food diary is removed outright                                                                |
| **« Améliore mon assiette »**       | The central V2 feature. The patient says what they are about to eat; REMI reads the meal, compares it with the practitioner's recommendations, proposes one concrete improvement, explains the why, and shifts habits gradually |
| **The daily hub — « hub du jour »** | Replaces the rigid meal plan with daily accompaniment and made-to-measure recipes                                                                                                                                               |
| **« Je mange autre chose »**        | The flexibility mode: the patient says what is actually available to them, and REMI generates an adapted alternative that stays compatible with the recommendations                                                             |
| **Smart supplement journal**        | The supplement follow-up, evolved: adapted reminders, better observance, and one standard across practitioners                                                                                                                  |
| **Micro-actions**                   | Not general advice — simple, immediate actions that take minutes to do                                                                                                                                                          |
| **The « pourquoi »**                | Every recommendation carries a clear explanation, so understanding and autonomy grow rather than compliance alone                                                                                                               |

Two constraints sit across all of them, from `priorities.md` and `court-term.md`: value delivered in
**under 60 seconds**, and a daily moment that is worth opening the app for.

## Practitioner — the V2 features

Parked until the patient experience is validated, and re-specified from terrain experience when it
is picked up. Listed here so the frozen scope is complete, not because it is next.

| Feature                       | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Practitioner dashboard**    | Patient follow-up, adherence at a glance, and quick identification of the patients who are struggling                                                                                          |
| **Per-patient detail view**   | Actions done, difficulties met, eating habits, and the patient's own feedback                                                                                                                  |
| **Adjusting recommendations** | Change the recommendations and the rules, and regenerate the accompaniment remotely                                                                                                            |
| **Quick feedback**            | Encouragements, emoji and short messages, to keep the motivational link alive between consultations                                                                                            |
| **Group messages**            | One piece of advice, a reminder or a teaching resource sent to several patients at once                                                                                                        |
| **Practitioner profile**      | A public profile, a content space and a small community around the practitioner — described in the braindump as a progressive build, and not named in the direction report's short freeze list |

## The technical centrepiece — the recommendation parser

`court-term.md` names it as the priority technical objective: turn a practitioner's own documents
into concrete accompaniment rules. It must read a PDF, extract the recommendations, structure them
as dietary rules, and personalise the advice automatically. Every patient feature above depends on
having those rules, which is why it is scope rather than infrastructure.

## Explicitly out of V2

Named here so they are not re-added. The first two were reconstructed from the deleted v1 and
appear nowhere in the braindump's V2; the next two existed in v1 and are dropped on purpose; the
rest are the braindump's own long term, which is not this build.

| Out of scope                      | Why                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Psychological questionnaire**   | A v1 reconstruction. Not in the braindump's V2                                                                                                                                               |
| **Nutrigenomics engine**          | A v1 reconstruction. Genetics appears in the braindump only as the far-future « REMI Genetics » idea; genotype personalisation is being explored commercially, but no engine is in the build |
| **Rigid weekly plan generation**  | Deliberately replaced. The daily hub is what supersedes it                                                                                                                                   |
| **7-day food diary**              | Deliberately killed. It is the single biggest source of v1's up-front effort, and removing it is stated as a V2 requirement                                                                  |
| Practitioner network and search   | The braindump's long term, not V2                                                                                                                                                            |
| Health marketplace                | The braindump's long term, not V2                                                                                                                                                            |
| Sleep, activity, stress, hormones | Multidisciplinary accompaniment is the braindump's long term, not V2                                                                                                                         |
| Behavioural-intelligence AI       | The braindump's long term, not V2                                                                                                                                                            |
| Wearables and health-system links | The braindump's long term, not V2                                                                                                                                                            |

The v1 feature list in `features.md` — plan generation, the food journal, recipe generation, the
supplement journal, progressive adaptation — is a record of what was built, not a specification.
V2 is not a port of v1.

## What is not decided

A stage that needs one of these takes the answer from the owner, not from this page.

- **Prices.** The braindump's practitioner tiers and patient premium are written as _réflexions_,
  not decisions. No figure appears on this site, and none is settled — see
  [`braindump/business/pricing.md`][pricing].
- **A V2 launch date.** The braindump's mid-2026 target passed. The 1 and 19 December dates above
  are the first patient version's milestones, not a V2 launch.

## How the pipeline uses this page

Scope checks a request against the tables above before writing a scope document; Define cites the
feature it is specifying. A request that is not on the patient list is not automatically a no — it
is a question for the owner, and the answer is a change to this page.
