# One REMI, wherever you land

_3 August 2026_

REMI now looks like one product from whichever door you come in. The name, the mark and the one-line
description of what REMI does are set in a single place and used by every part of the product — the
signed-in app, the public site, the help centre, the operator console and this reference site.

What you will notice:

- The same wordmark in the same typeface at the top of every screen, at the sizes it is meant to
  appear at and no others.
- A REMI icon in your browser tab and on your phone's home screen, in place of the blank page icon.
- A proper preview card when a REMI link is shared in a message or on social media, instead of a
  bare URL.
- One description of REMI, in English and in French, written once and repeated exactly — so the
  sentence you read on the public site is the sentence you read everywhere else.

Nothing you can do in REMI changed. This is the groundwork that stops the product looking like
several products, which matters most while the first practitioners are still deciding whether to
trust it.
