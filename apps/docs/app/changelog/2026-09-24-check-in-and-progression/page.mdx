# Once a week, each goal gets a number from 0 to 5

_24 September 2026_

Between two consultations, the person now tells you how their week went, goal by goal, on the
scale you asked for — and you both see where it is heading.

What that changes for you:

- **They answer on their home, once a week.** When their last answer is a week old, their home
  asks « Cette semaine, comment ça s'est passé pour : … ? » for each goal you set, from 0 (pas bien
  du tout) to 5 (très bien), with room for a word. A goal can be left without a number. Once they
  have answered, the card says when, and when the next question opens. Nothing is sent to them —
  the question waits for them to open their link.
- **They see their progression.** A new « Ma progression » page shows each goal's numbers week
  after week, the consigne you gave them, how many meals they logged in the last seven days, and
  the challenges already behind them with how each ended.
- **You see the same numbers.** On the patient page, the goals in the working view carry the same
  row of weekly numbers, and each answer appears in the goal's trail with its number and
  « écrit depuis le lien ».
- **A drop comes to you.** When a number is lower than the one they gave the week before, the
  patient page says so — « 1 objectif en baisse attend votre regard » — until you press « Vu » on
  that answer in the goal's trail. Your own check-ins never count there.
