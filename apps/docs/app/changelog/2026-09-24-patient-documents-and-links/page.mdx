# Your PDFs and links, on the person's page

_24 September 2026_

The recipe PDFs you send over WhatsApp, the personalised list of fifteen foods, a meal feedback, a
useful link — they can now live on the person's own page, where they find them without scrolling
back through a conversation.

What that changes for you:

- **You add them from the patient page.** Under « Documents », upload a PDF or an image (JPEG, PNG
  or WebP, up to 10 MB) or add a link, with the title the person will read. Class it as a recipe or
  as a document, and if it belongs with one of their goals or one of their recipes, attach it
  there. You can change the title, the class and the attachment later, or remove it — a removed
  file is deleted, not hidden.
- **They find them in « Documents ».** Newest first, each with the day you added it. A file opens
  in their browser; a link opens in a new tab. The newest few also show on their home.
- **Your recipe PDFs sit with their recipes.** Anything you class as a recipe is also listed under
  « Recettes », beside the recipes you assign in REMI. A document attached to a recipe shows under
  that recipe, and one attached to a goal shows under the goal on their home.
- **You can see the page as they see it.** « Voir comme la patiente » opens their link in a new
  tab in one click — and your visit is not counted as theirs opening it.

The files stay private: nothing has a public address, each file opens through a link that expires
after five minutes, and only the person's own link can open theirs. Deleting a person deletes their
files along with the rest.
