# Your patients keep their own food profile up to date

_25 September 2026_

The patient link has a new page, « Mon profil ». Your patients can now change the things only they
really know: their diet, allergies, intolerances, the foods they like and dislike, whether they
like cooking, how much time they have to cook and their food budget. The last two are simple
choices — faible, moyen or important for time; économique, standard or confort for budget — the
same words the first version used.

On their profile in the console, every field a patient changed carries « Modifié par la patiente
le … », so an allergy they added or removed never goes unnoticed. The note comes off a field as
soon as you change that field yourself. The patient form in the console has the same two choices
for time and budget, and the context you copy for a prompt now includes both.

Budgets you had typed as free text were kept: where the text was exactly one of the three levels
it became that level, and anything else was added to the patient's likes and dislikes as
« Budget : … », so you can pick the level again.

Below the form, your patients see what you have on record about them — their name, age, height
and weight — and nothing more.
