# One habit at a time, and you see when it sticks

_23 September 2026_

« Boire 1,5 L d'eau par jour », « Manger plus lentement », « Ajouter une source de protéines au
petit-déjeuner » — the habits you run with each person one at a time now live in REMI, and so do
their two answers to them.

What that changes for you:

- **You launch a challenge from the patient page.** One sentence written to the person, an
  optional reason, a start date. You can reword it while it runs, and close it as « Acquis »,
  « Non acquis » or « Abandonné ». Launching the next one while one is running asks how the
  current one ended, then does both at once — they are never left with none, or with two.
- **They see it first.** It is the first thing on their link, under « Aujourd'hui / cette
  semaine ». When nothing is running, the page says so rather than leaving a gap.
- **They tell you when it has stuck.** Two taps: « Challenge acquis », then — once that is set —
  « Prêt(e) pour le prochain ». They can undo either until you close the challenge.
- **You see it without opening WhatsApp.** The patient list carries a badge when someone is ready
  for the next challenge, or has marked the current one acquired. The patient page shows the
  running challenge with the dates of both answers, and says when they are waiting for the next
  one. Past challenges keep their outcome and what the person said, folded at the foot of the
  section.

One word changes on both sides so the two things never share a name: what the link used to call
« Le challenge de la semaine » is now « La consigne de la semaine » — the same text you write in the
consigne's patient field, under its own name, below the challenge.
