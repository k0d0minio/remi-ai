# The profile closes when you save it

_18 September 2026_

« Modifier » on a patient's profile opens the form, and a « Fermer » above it used to close the form
again. It sat at the top of five hundred lines of fields — the one control you could reach without
scrolling — and it closed the form whether or not you had typed into it. Everything you had typed
went with it, without a word.

« Fermer » is gone, and saving is the way back. « Enregistrer les modifications » writes the profile
and returns you to the summary, which now shows what you just entered and the moment you entered it.
Nothing in the form itself changed, and there is no longer a control that can throw away a page of
encoding on one click.
