# Recipes, written and given where you are

_17 September 2026_

Giving someone a recipe used to mean leaving their page, writing the recipe somewhere else, coming
back, and handing it over one at a time. And if a recipe nearly suited someone — the right dish,
the wrong ingredient — there was no good answer: editing it changed it for everyone who had it.

All three now happen on the person's own page:

- **Write a recipe and give it in the same save.** « Proposer une recette » opens the form right
  there, and saving both adds it to your library and gives it to the person you are with. It is
  offered even when the library is empty, so the first recipe is written where you need it.
- **Adapt one for a single person.** « Dupliquer en variante » copies a recipe, lets you change it
  freely, gives the new version to that person and moves the old one into their previous recipes.
  Everyone else keeps exactly what they were given. The copy says which recipe it came from, and
  links back to it.
- **Give several at once.** Tick the recipes you want from the library, write one note and one
  date, and save once. A recipe someone already has is refused by name before anything is written,
  so you can fix the selection rather than hunt for what went wrong.

The library is unchanged and still shared: a recipe written from a person's page lands in it like
any other, and a variant is a new recipe in it rather than a private copy.
