# One place for how their week is going

_24 September 2026_

« Comment se passe votre accompagnement cette semaine ? » — the question you asked on WhatsApp now
has a home on each person's page, and their answers reach you in REMI.

What that changes for you:

- **They write to you from their link.** Your question, word for word, sits on their home and
  under « Messages », with one box to answer it — how the week went, and what they thought of the
  recipes, the challenges or the recommendations. The page tells them the day they last wrote; it
  never nags by email or notification.
- **You see who is waiting.** The patient list carries a badge — « 2 messages attendent une
  réponse » — and the patient page shows the same count with the latest messages, unread ones
  marked.
- **You answer in the same thread.** Your reply appears on their page under your name, and marks
  everything they had sent before it as read. A message that needs no answer can be cleared with
  « Marquer comme lu ».

One place, on purpose: there are no comments on individual recipes or recommendations. Messages
are never edited or deleted, and the thread goes with the person if their profile is deleted.
