# The meal journal, out of the chat thread

_3 September 2026_

Until now, a patient sent a photo of their lunch, got an answer back, and both disappeared into a
messaging thread. Three weeks later nobody could find the correction, and what the month had taught
about someone lived only in memory.

The console now holds that exchange.

What you will notice:

- A journal on each patient's page: what they ate, the day it was eaten, and your reply underneath
  it — read as a conversation rather than two lists to line up by eye.
- Adding a meal takes two fields. The date is already filled in and can be moved back, because
  Tuesday's lunch often gets written up on Thursday. The comment, the reply and the note to
  yourself are one tap away when you want them, and out of sight when you don't.
- Meals still waiting for a reply are marked, and counted at the top of the journal.
- A « what to remember » list per patient, gathering both the notes you made on a particular meal
  and the ones about the person that belong to no single meal — a habit you keep noticing, a dish
  that always works.

Photos stay in the messaging thread for now. Everything here is text you type, and nothing from the
journal appears on the link you share with a patient.
