# Priority goals and the standing instruction

**Who it's for:** Morgane, in the operator console.
**What shipped:** She sets a patient's two or three priority goals in order, records a dated check-in against each, and keeps one standing instruction that archives when she replaces it.
**Why it matters:** A real database behind the surfaces: real accounts, real records, real recommendations.
