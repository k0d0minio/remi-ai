# Breakdown: recipe-space — REMI proposes the patient's recipes, every day, inside the validated protocol

- epic-slug: recipe-space
- source: story 24 « Espace Recettes », Morgane Paquet, 15 October 2026, https://drive.google.com/file/d/10EWwQ9BGJTd-hrdJX_P-ztY-BqCVdpBW/view
- story: 24

## What I understood

The patient opens « Recettes » and finds today's proposals, one recipe per enabled meal, generated by REMI from the protocol the practitioner validated. REMI prepares seven days ahead, shows only today, varies the week, follows the season, and regenerates the future days when a validated version changes. No recipe reaches the patient before the compatibility gate confirms it against allergies, intolerances and total evictions. An uncertain recipe is excluded.

On `main`, `recipes` is Morgane's hand-written library (prose body, tags) and `patient_recipe_assignments` is her weekly hand assignment. The story replaces that loop with generation. Under D-34 every generation starts from a verified template recipe and adapts it to the protocol. The library is the natural home of the templates (question 3 for Jamie). The ai seam exists with three model roles and the pseudonymous context block (`packages/services/src/ai/context.ts`). No adapter is registered yet. Stub 1 builds it. Stories 28 and 29 depend on that stub. Morgane filed the v1 prompts in Drive on 24 October 2026 (`Prompt.docx`, `01-weekly-planner.md`, `02-single-meal.md`). They are reference for the prompt's tone and structure, never a spec: the stories and D-30 win where they differ. The macronutrient split a recipe follows is `genotype-macro-split` (story 10): generation reads the validated split only.

This epic rests on D-16 (gateway, structured output, log, safety in code), D-26 (the screen lives behind the patient account on `apps/web`), D-30 (story 30's priority order) and D-18 (files). It rests on D-34 too: a verified template is the start of every generation, never a blank prompt. It supersedes D-5 (« the library stays » as the patient's source): the patient reads generated recipes, never the library directly. It retires the token segment `[locale]/p/[token]/recettes`.

### Questions for Morgane

1. Which recipes form the template base REMI starts from (D-34), and how many are verified before 1 December? Default: your library, grown to more than twenty, each one verified by you.
2. Your « Calendrier fruits et légumes de saison » (Drive, 23 October 2026) is the season table REMI follows. Correct? Default: yes, loaded once as data, month by month.
3. How far back must REMI avoid a repeat? Default: no repeat in the week and none from the previous two weeks.
4. Do « aime cuisiner », « temps disponible » and « budget » still constrain the recipes? Default: yes, as soft preferences below priority 4 of story 30.
5. The patient sees today only, so a shopping list covers at most today's recipes. Is that intended? Default: yes, today only.
6. For how many people does REMI generate a recipe before the patient scales it? Default: one portion.
7. The story asks for a justification « si facile à mettre en place sinon pas pour le MVP ». It costs one field in the generation schema. Build it now? Default: yes.

### Questions for Jamie

1. Which role generates a recipe? Default: `balanced` for the recipe, `fast` for the checks. Gateway model ids replace the Anthropic ids in `ai/index.ts`.
2. One call per recipe or one call per day? Default: one call per recipe. The schema stays small and a failed gate retries one recipe.
3. Where do generated recipes and the templates live? Default: new tables `patient_recipes` and `patient_meal_plan` for the generated recipes. `recipes` holds the templates and gains the structured shape and a `verified` flag in `recipe-structure`.
4. The daily top-up: a Vercel cron route on `apps/web`? Default: yes, one idempotent route at 03:00 Europe/Brussels.
5. Story 33 removed the meal photo from story 28. Only story 29 (the menu photo, bonus) still reads an image. Add an image input to the seam now? Default: no. `restaurant-menu/menu-photo-reading` adds it when it starts.

## Build order

1. ai-gateway-adapter — the adapter behind the ai seam, `generateJson`, the `ai_generations` log, the env row — depends-on: none
2. recipe-structure — the structured recipe, the verified template and the seven-day plan tables — depends-on: ai-gateway-adapter
3. weekly-plan-generation — seven days of recipes from verified templates and the validated protocol, through the gate, varied and seasonal, regenerated on a new version — depends-on: recipe-structure
4. patient-recipes-today — the patient's « Recettes » screen, today only, by meal — depends-on: weekly-plan-generation
5. recipe-justification — the short « pourquoi pour toi » on each recipe — depends-on: weekly-plan-generation

Stubs 1 and 2 both add migrations. They run in this order and never in parallel (D-25).

## Out of scope

- The recipe card, portions, favourites and sharing: `recipe-card` (story 25).
- The shopping list: `shopping-list` (story 26).
- The compatibility gate itself: `eviction-safety/compatibility-gate`. This epic calls it.
- Meal structure and fasting as inputs: `meal-structure` (story 11), `intermittent-fasting` (story 12).
- The practitioner's view of today's recipes: `practitioner-daily-recipes` (story 32).
- Embeddings, agents, tool use, a second adapter. One structured call per recipe is the whole design until the log says otherwise.
