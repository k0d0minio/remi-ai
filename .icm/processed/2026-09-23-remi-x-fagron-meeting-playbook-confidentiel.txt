MORGANE — SAY
Nobody likes change. Changing habits, especially food habits, is difficult.

We also saw this very clearly in the field. Knowing that you are APOE2 can be useful, but the real question is: what does that actually change in my breakfast, my lunch or my dinner?
Knowing what to change is not enough. People need help applying it every day.
Our ambition with REMI is simple: make change easier, step by step.
And maybe, if changing becomes easier, people will also be more willing to know themselves better, do biological or genetic tests, and act earlier for their health.
This is also why FunMedDev trusted us as our first clinical partner. They are a functional medicine clinic in Liège, and they were facing the same problem with their patients. We have an ongoing collaboration with them to test our MVPs, starting with around 30 patients.
