## The seed

Nine draft rows, inserted by the same migration that creates the table — migrations run once, so
the seed needs no script and no environment variable, and it is guarded on an empty table so a
manual re-apply cannot duplicate it. **Every row is `draft`.** Five carry real content quoted from
the v2 structure brainstorm (§§ G, H, I) with the source line in the body; four are skeletons
marked « à compléter » for the food lists § H names and never fills — oméga-3, fibres, polyphénols,
diversité végétale.

The spec's finding held up in the build: **`.icm/docs/braindump/` contains no nutrition content.**
A grep for oméga-3, polyphénols, fibres, magnésium and anti-inflammatoire across all seven of its
folders returns nothing; they are business, marketing, roadmap, vision and journal material. The
whole of what there was to seed is the brainstorm and the correspondence.

## Notes for Release

- **Smoke-test order on the preview.** Create a rule → validate it → edit it (this must land you on
  a _new_ page at version 2, with the old one still reading « validée ») → validate the new version
  → archive and restore it. The version links at the top of the detail page are the quickest way to
  see the chain is intact.
- **The deliberate gap.** Between a revision and its validation, neither row is retrievable. The
  form says so above the button, and a test asserts it. Worth confirming that is the behaviour
  Morgane wants — the alternative (keep the old wording live until the new one is validated) is a
  small change to `updateNutritionRule`, not a reshape.
- **The seed only appears where migrations run.** A preview without `DATABASE_URL` skips them, and
  `migrate.mjs` refuses to migrate from a non-production Vercel deploy. On a preview pointed at a
  migrated database the nine drafts will be there; on one without, `/knowledge` renders its empty
  state, which is correct rather than broken.
- **`react-markdown` under RSC.** Its module imports `useEffect`/`useState` for `MarkdownHooks`,
  which the `react-server` build of React does not export; the `Markdown` export we use is
  synchronous and calls neither. Bundlers bind the unused specifiers to `undefined` and warn. If
  the preview turns that into a hard error, the fix is one line — drop the two from `external` in
  `packages/ui/tsup.config.ts` so esbuild tree-shakes `MarkdownHooks` and the imports with it.
- **Two open questions are still open, by design** (spec § Open questions): her tag vocabulary, and
  whether the corpus is house-wide or per-practitioner. Neither blocks anything here. The four
  « à compléter » rows are the first thing to put in front of her.
