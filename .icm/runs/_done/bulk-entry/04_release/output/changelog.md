# A whole protocol in one save, not thirty

_17 September 2026_

Encoding a protocol used to cost one save per line. Eight recommendations, five supplements and a
dozen essentials meant about thirty round-trips, each one a page cycle before you could type the
next thing. That is the reason a profile took far too long to encode, and it is what changes here.

Each of the three lists now has a **Modifier** button that opens the whole section at once:

- **Recommandations** open as one block per category — nutrition, habitudes, activité, suivi —
  with the lines under each. You work through a category the way you write it, and there is no
  category to pick per line.
- **Compléments** open as every prescribed line on screen together: nom and dose on the row, with
  moment and pourquoi a click away so the quick part stays quick.
- **Essentiels** open the same way, and take a pasted list: drop in a dozen lines from your own
  notes and each one becomes a line on the list, ready to edit or drop before you save.

In all three you can add a line, remove one, and move lines up and down as much as you like —
nothing is written until you press **Enregistrer la section**. Then it all goes at once, and the
journal records one entry for the save rather than one per line.

If something goes wrong mid-save, nothing is written: the section stays exactly as it was and your
lines stay on screen to try again. A line you take out is archived, not deleted, so « pourquoi
est-ce qu'on a arrêté ça » still has an answer.

The one-line forms are still there under each list, for when you are on your phone and want to add
a single thing.
