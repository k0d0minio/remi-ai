# The rest of the record, made readable

_18 September 2026_

The working view answered the questions you ask at the start of a consultation. Everything behind
it still read like the database it came from: twelve anamnesis headings whether or not you had
filled any of them, a five-hundred-line form standing between you and a consent date, and a page
whose shape changed depending on how much a person had archived.

- **The anamnesis shows what you know first.** The areas you have written up come first, with their
  text. The ones you have not are a short row of prompts underneath, and a line above says how many
  of the twelve are in. Completing one opens it where you are and moves it up when you save.
- **The profile is something to read.** Identity, measures, food and consent are laid out as text,
  with the date it was last changed. « Modifier » brings the form back, whole and unchanged, for
  when you are actually encoding — and nothing was removed from it.
- **What is no longer live folds away, and says how much.** Archived recommendations, essentials,
  meals, observations and goals, stopped supplements and previous recipes each sit closed at the
  foot of the section they belong to, labelled with their count. Seven places that each looked
  different now look the same, and four sections that only appeared for some people are gone.
- **Deleting a profile is at the end of the profile.** It folds closed like the rest, instead of
  standing as a red panel at the bottom of the page. The confirmation it asks for has not changed.

The page now holds the same thirteen sections for everybody, so the index, the anchors and the
phone segments read the same on a new patient as on one you have followed for months.
