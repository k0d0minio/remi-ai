# Writing up a consultation, in one go

_17 September 2026_

After a session you updated five things in five places: the note, each goal, the instruction of the
moment, the living summary, and what to prepare for next time. Five cards, five saves, and three of
them on different tabs of the patient page.

There is now one screen for it. The first quick action on a patient's page, « Nouvelle
consultation », opens the write-up in the order the work happens, and one button saves all of it.

What you will notice:

- The note first — date, title and what you want to write down — then each active goal with its
  check-in beside the last one you recorded, so you can see what you are moving from.
- The instruction and the living summary come up filled in with what is currently in force. Change
  what needs changing; anything you leave alone is left alone.
- The recommendations, supplements and essentials stay in their own sections — the screen links
  straight to them and brings you back where you were.
- What you type is kept as you go. Follow a link, close the tab by accident, come back: it is still
  there, and it is only on your own device. It clears itself once you save, and after twelve hours
  if you never do.
- One save, one line in the journal — « consultation enregistrée » — and you land back on the
  patient's working view with everything showing.
