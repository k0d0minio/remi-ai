# The patient link is no longer read-only

_17 September 2026_

The link you send a patient has always shown them their recommendations, their essentials and
their recipes. It could not take anything back. That changes here — not as a form they can fill in
yet, but as the foundation every one of those forms will sit on.

What is in place now:

- Anything a patient writes through their link is saved as **theirs**, not as yours. The meal
  journal and the goal check-ins now record who wrote each row, so you can tell your own
  transcription from their words at a glance — and so can anything that reads the record later.
- Your patient page shows « dernière écriture » beside « dernière ouverture ». They are different
  questions: one tells you they looked, the other tells you something is waiting for you.
- A patient writing into their link no longer reorders your roster. That list still means "who I
  last worked on", which is what makes it useful.
- The journal names a patient's write as a patient's write, rather than showing their name the way
  it shows yours.

And what your patients now read on their own page: that what they write is kept, that you see it,
and — plainly — that anyone holding their link can write as them. The link is still the whole key,
so they should pass it on only to people they choose. If one goes astray, replacing it from the
patient page kills the old one immediately.

Nothing a patient can click has arrived yet. « Je vais manger », « J'ai mangé », the check-in and
the recipe feedback are next, and they all land on this.
