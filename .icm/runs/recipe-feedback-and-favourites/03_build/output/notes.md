## What changed

- `packages/services/src/shared/recipe.ts`: `recipeResponses` — the four stable ASCII keys — and
  `FAVOURITE_RESPONSE`, so no call site spells out that « À refaire » is the favourite.
- `packages/services/src/db/schema.ts` + `migrations/0020_dizzy_vengeance.sql`: three additive
  columns on `patient_recipe_assignments` — `patient_response`, `responded_at`, `written_by`
  (default `practitioner`). Generated with `pnpm db:generate` **after** merging `main`, per the
  regenerate-never-renumber rule.
- `packages/services/src/db/services/recipe-assignments/`: `respondToRecipeAssignment` (one of the
  four, or `null` to take it back), `recipeAssignmentOwner` (the ownership answer the write helper
  needs before a token may touch a row it named by id), `listPatientRecipeFavourites` (every
  « à refaire », archived included, newest answer first with the giving order as tie-break).
  Every existing insert carries the three new columns from birth through one `unanswered` literal.
- `packages/services/src/shared/audit.ts`: `recipe.response_written` / `recipe.response_cleared`,
  a pair like `meal.feedback_*` so "they took it back" survives in the trail.
- `apps/web/lib/patient-link/actions.ts`: `respondToRecipeAction` — posts through
  `writePatientLink`, so the token is resolved as the loader resolves it, the ceilings apply, and
  the row is audited with a patient actor. `""` is the clear.
- `apps/web/components/patient-link/recipe-response.tsx`: the client island — `useOptimistic` so
  the tap lands before the round-trip, `aria-pressed` for the state, the refusal rendered in place.
  Buttons rather than the meal form's `ChoiceChip` because a radio cannot be unchecked by pressing
  it again, which is the clearing gesture.
- `apps/web/components/patient-link/recipe-list.tsx` + `recettes/page.tsx`: the shelf first with
  its own heading, the active list under it carrying the buttons. `titleAs` keeps the heading
  outline intact now that the segment has two levels.
- `apps/web/lib/patient-link/load.ts`: `favourites` read alongside `recipes`, and the `recettes`
  segment now also appears when only the shelf has something — otherwise archiving a favourite
  would make it unreachable, which is archiving acting as a delete.
- `apps/web/lib/content/{types,fr,en}.ts`: `recipeFeedback` — her four words verbatim in FR,
  mirrored in EN, plus the shelf's title and lead and one error sentence per `PatientWriteError`.
- `apps/admin/`: the answer and its date on the assignment row, an « Avis sur les recettes » count
  line under « À retenir » (archived givings included), French labels and intents in
  `patients/vocabulary.ts`, and the two new actions worded in `audit/vocabulary.ts`.
